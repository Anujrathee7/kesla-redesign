## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui on Radix primitives, same setup as the
CraftLeap redesign (`projects/craftleap`). Finnish and English with a language toggle from the
start. Local build first; a Vercel deploy for the pitch is likely but not decided. Pages are
redesigned one at a time, starting with the frontpage. For each page, 3 or 4 directions are shown
in a Claude artifact first, Anuj picks one, and the build follows the chosen direction.

## Users

Primary: machine buyers. Forest contractors, farmers and forest owners, timber and city-crane truck
operators, comparing cranes, grapples, harvester heads, loaders and trailers before contacting a
dealer. Many arrive on a phone, some from outside Finland (53% of turnover is export).

Secondary: dealers looking for catalogues, spare parts and contacts, job seekers (Career), and
investors (Kesla is listed). The pitch reader is Kesla itself, who will see this as an unsolicited
redesign from a job applicant (Anuj Rathee).

## Product Purpose

kesla.com presents Kesla Oyj's machinery and routes a buyer to a local dealer, a catalogue or a
factory contact. Success means a buyer finds the right product family fast, understands what
separates the models, and reaches a dealer.

The redesign keeps the original Kesla look and feel and fixes the mess: the cramped two-row nav,
the sidebar of news and events squeezing the main column, the six same-size highlight tiles with
no order of importance, the floating chat widget over content, and the footer that mixes video,
social icons and logos.

## Positioning

A Finnish family company since 1960, founded by farmer Antti Kärkkäinen, that designs and builds
its own forest and material-handling machinery in North Karelia. Tagline: "Your responsible
partner." Site copy: "innovative, high-quality professional machinery, made in Finland with decades
of experience and customer understanding, help you to succeed, in and outside the forest."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Finnish and English in the redesign, with a toggle. The other six languages are out of scope for
  the pitch.
- Product data, specs and dealer lists come from kesla.com. No invented models or figures.
- Imagery is Kesla's own photography from kesla.com, used for a pitch mockup.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the original design and feel. Anuj's call, 18 October 2026. This is a refinement of the
  Kesla identity, not a new visual world.
- KESLA wordmark logo (slanted, with the red/black slash mark), both standard and inverted.
- Kesla yellow #FEB825, black and near-black #1A1A1A, body grey #636363, light grey #F7F7F7, warm
  grey #F0EFEC, with a red accent #F1144A.
- Exo / Exo 2 for headings (uppercase for labels), Frutiger for body.
- Chamfered (cut) corners on panels and the yellow slanted band under the hero.
- Machine photography with black-and-white treatment on secondary tiles.
- Voice: plain, confident, practical. "Your responsible partner."

## Evidence on Hand

- Founded 1960. Turnover 2025 €34.4 million, 53% export. About 200 employees. Production in
  Joensuu (HQ), Kesälahti and Ilomantsi, sales office Kesla GmbH in Appenweier, Germany. Family
  owned, A shares listed on Nasdaq Helsinki.
- Original page screenshots: `original/`.
- Absent, must not be fabricated: customer testimonials or quotes, sales volumes, model counts,
  awards, dealer counts.

## Product Principles

1. Kesla first, redesign second: anyone at Kesla should recognise their own brand at a glance.
2. Products lead: the three product families are the main road from the frontpage.
3. Every page ends at a person: a dealer, a factory contact or a catalogue.
4. Order over volume: fewer things on screen, in a clear order of importance.
5. Truth over polish: only real products, facts and Kesla's own images.
